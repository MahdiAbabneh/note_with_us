PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Note With Us is a private journal and a small social space for close friends and family (ages 13+). People write short notes about their day and tag a mood, keep them private, share them only with a chosen "circle", or post them publicly. They can also seal "time capsules" (notes that unlock on a future date). It helps people reflect on how they feel and stay close to the people they care about, without the pressure of a large social network.

HOW TO USE THE APP
- Demo account: use the credentials in Sign-In Information. You can also tap "Look around first" on the welcome screen to browse public notes without an account.
- Write a note: tap the pink + button, type, pick a mood, choose Only me / Circle / Everyone, then Post.
- Journal and capsules: the book tab shows private notes, time capsules and favorites. Capsules open on their date.
- Your week, calendar and mood report: Today tab > "Your week".
- Messages and circle: chat tab. Add people to your circle from Me > My circle.
- Settings: Me tab (language Arabic/English, theme, reminders).

ACCOUNT CREATION AND DELETION
- Create an account: welcome screen > Continue with email > Create account (Sign in with Apple and Google are also available).
- Delete account: Me tab > scroll down > Delete account. This permanently deletes the profile and the user's notes.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT: REPORTING AND BLOCKING
- Report a note: tap "..." on any note > Report note.
- Report or block a person: tap "..." on their note > Block, or open their profile > Report / Block.
- Blocked people: Me > Blocked people (unblock from there). Blocked users cannot message you and their notes are hidden.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Firebase Authentication (email, Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In)
- Cloud Firestore (stores notes, messages and profiles)
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications)
No payments, ads, analytics or AI services are used.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. It is available in English and Arabic.
